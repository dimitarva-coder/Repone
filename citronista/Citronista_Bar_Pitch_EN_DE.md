# Citronista: email pitch for bars (English and German)

**Fill in before sending:** `[First name]`, `[Bar name]`, `[a drink from their menu]`, `[Your name]`, `[phone/WhatsApp]`. Optional: `[X litres]` per bag-in-box.

**Tips**
- Send to the **bar manager or owner by name**. Find them on the bar's Instagram or website.
- Send the German version to German bars. If the bar's Instagram is in English (common in Berlin), the English version is fine.
- Mention **one drink from their menu**. It shows you looked, and generic emails get ignored.
- Best time to send: **Tuesday to Thursday, 11:00–14:00**, when the bar team is awake but not yet in service.
