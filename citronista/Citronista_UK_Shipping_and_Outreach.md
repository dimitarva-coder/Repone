# Citronista: shipping to the UK and UK outreach

*Researched 23 Nov 2026. Prices change often, so get live quotes with your real box weights and sizes before you quote a customer.*

## 1. The short answer

| What you're sending | Best option | Rough cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Samples (1–2 boxes)** | **Ask Speedy first** (it's part of the DPD group, the same network as DPD UK). Then compare **Eurosender**, **Packlink** and **ParcelBroker** | About £20–30 for a 10–20 kg parcel | ParcelBroker lists **20 kg BG→UK from £27.79 + VAT**. Send samples as "commercial sample, no charge" with an invoice. |
| **Small orders (2–8 boxes, up to ~70 kg)** | Speedy/DPD international or Eurosender, sent **DDP** (you pay UK VAT and duty, so the bar gets no surprise bill) | About £30–80 + customs fee | A UK bar that receives a "pay £X customs" card will not reorder. Always send DDP. |
| **Regular volume (a pallet: ~40–60 boxes)** | **Pallet groupage**: Eurosender groupage, Pallet2Ship, PalletOnline, Barrington Freight, or a Bulgarian haulier with weekly UK trucks | About **£165–200 per pallet** door to door (UK→BG examples), plus customs clearance | Ask each one for **Bulgaria → UK, 1 pallet, food, customs clearance both sides included**. |
| **Long term (best)** | Send **one pallet a month to a UK fulfilment warehouse (3PL)**, then deliver inside the UK with DPD UK / Royal Mail next-day | Groupage + about £6–12 per box inside the UK | One customs clearance a month instead of one per order. UK customers get **next-day delivery** and a UK price list, which beats 5–6 days from Bulgaria. |

**DHL Express** is fast but usually the most expensive. Only use it for urgent samples, or if you can get a negotiated business account. UPS and FedEx publish 2026 Bulgaria export tariffs and are similar.

### Next steps (about 1 hour)
1. **Call your Speedy account manager.** Ask: *"Do you ship to the United Kingdom through DPD? What is the price for a 20 kg and a 60 kg parcel, and can you handle the export customs declaration and DDP?"* You already have an account, so you may get a contract rate.
2. Get instant quotes on **eurosender.com** (Bulgaria → United Kingdom), **packlink.com** and **parcelbroker.co.uk** (import Bulgaria → UK) for the same weight and size.
3. For pallets, get 2–3 quotes, including Speedy's own **"Pallet One EU"** service. Ask whether it covers the UK.

## 2. UK customs and rules (since Brexit the UK is outside the EU)

Check these with a customs broker or your courier before the first commercial shipment:

- **EORI:** you need your Bulgarian EORI to export. The UK importer (you via a UK entity or agent, or the customer) needs a **GB EORI**.
- **Commercial invoice + HS code:** lemon/lime juice is in **HS 2009** (citrus juices: 2009 31 / 2009 39 depending on Brix). Confirm the exact 10-digit code on the UK Trade Tariff (gov.uk/trade-tariff).
- **Import duty:** under the EU–UK trade agreement, goods of **EU origin pay 0% duty** if you put a **statement on origin** on the invoice. Check that your juice qualifies as EU origin under the rules of origin for heading 2009, especially if the lemons are grown outside the EU. If it doesn't qualify, the UK tariff for juice codes can be around **6–8% plus a specific duty**.
- **Import VAT 20%:** in the UK, **fruit juice is standard-rated** (HMRC VAT Notice 701/14). Lemon juice sold in retail packs purely for cooking can be zero-rated, but juice sold to bars for drinks should be treated as 20%. VAT-registered UK buyers can usually reclaim it.
- **Orders of £135 or less:** for consignments of this size, the **seller** charges UK VAT at the point of sale. If the buyer is VAT-registered and gives you their VAT number, they account for the VAT themselves (reverse charge). Larger orders: import VAT is paid at the border, so ship **DDP** and add it to your price.
- **Food controls:** processed plant-based foods from the EU are low risk. A new **UK–EU SPS (food safety) agreement** is being put in place in 2026 to reduce paperwork. Customs declarations still apply.
- **Labels:** pre-packed food sold in Great Britain needs a **UK food business name and address** (an importer or distributor) on the label. Confirm this for bag-in-box sold to trade.

**Simplest way to handle all of this:** appoint a **UK distributor** (see the "P5 Distributors" rows in the lead list: Matthew Clark, LWC, Speciality Brands, Mangrove, Love Drinks and others). The distributor becomes the importer of record, deals with VAT and labels, and delivers next day across the UK.

## 3. The UK lead list (`Citronista_200_Leads_UK.xlsx`)

- **200 leads:** 60 cocktail bars (the UK Top 50 2026 plus busy city bars), 61 bar and restaurant chains, 23 hotel groups and resorts, 13 juice/café chains, 27 distributors, wholesalers and RTD/keg-cocktail makers, and 16 caterers and event bars.
- **85 have a contact email** taken from a public listing or the venue's own website. The source is in the "Contact source" column. **11** have only a staff email pattern. **104** need the website form, phone or LinkedIn. Big chains almost never publish buyer emails, so for them find the **Head of Drinks / Bar Development / Purchasing** on LinkedIn.
- Rows marked **⚠ in red** may be closed, moved or in administration. Check them before sending.
- **Start with these four groups:** distributors (one "yes" covers hundreds of venues), RTD and keg-cocktail makers (the alcohol-stable angle is unique to you), caterers and event bars (no squeezing on site), and juice/café chains.

### Changes to the English pitch for the UK
Use the English email from `Citronista_Bar_Pitch_EN_DE.md`, with these changes:
- Replace *"Bag-in-box by courier in 5–6 working days, free delivery from 8 boxes"* with what you can deliver once shipping is set up. For example: **"Delivered to your door, duties paid. No customs paperwork for you."** Once you hold UK stock: **"Next-day UK delivery."**
- Quote prices in **£ (GBP) excluding VAT**. UK buyers expect this.
- Add one line: **"Can I send you a free sample box this week?"** This gets more replies than a general offer.
- UK spelling: "flavour", "colour", "litre".
