# Citronista: email pitches for hotels and restaurants (English and German)

**Fill in before sending:** `[First name]` / `[Last name]`, `[Hotel name]` / `[Restaurant name]`, `[Name]`, `[phone/WhatsApp]`.

**Tips**
- Most addresses on the list are general inboxes (info@, reservations). Every email therefore **asks them to forward it to the F&B manager or purchasing (Einkauf)**.
- Better still, find the **F&B Manager** or **Einkaufsleiter/in** on LinkedIn and send to them by name.
- Hotels and chains are more formal than bars. The German versions use **Sie**, not "ihr".
