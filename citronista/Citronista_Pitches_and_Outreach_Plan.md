# Citronista: pitches and outreach plan for Germany

I couldn't open citronista.com from here, so these templates don't make claims about your product that I can't check. Buyers reply to **specific numbers**, not adjectives.

---

## 1. Why 50 emails got 2 replies (and what to change)

A 4% reply rate on a first cold email is normal. The ways to improve it:

1. **Write in German.** Most bar managers and purchasing people (Einkauf) read English, but a German email reads as a supplier who can serve them. The German versions are below each pitch.
2. **Send it to a person, not info@.** Look up the *Barmanager/in*, *F&B Manager/in* or *Einkauf* on the venue's Instagram, website or LinkedIn. A first name in the greeting roughly doubles replies.
3. **Follow up twice.** Most replies come from the 2nd or 3rd touch. Send follow-up 1 after 4 days and follow-up 2 after 10 days. The spreadsheet has columns for both.
4. **Lead with a free sample.** "Can I send you 2 bottles free, delivered to the door?" is a small yes. "Do you want to buy from us?" is a big one. The sample is your sales visit while you can't be in Germany.
5. **Ask one easy question.** End with "Shall I send a sample this week, yes or no?", not "let me know if interested".
6. **Use numbers.** Lead with cost per cocktail, minutes of prep saved per shift, shelf life and yield. Bar managers think in cost per serve.
7. **Ads vs DMs.** A one-week Instagram ad rarely makes B2B sales. Use Instagram as a direct-message channel to bar managers instead (template in section 9). Use ads later to retarget people who visited your site.
8. **Distributors multiply your reach.** One "yes" from a Getränke-Großhändler puts you in front of hundreds of venues, and they handle invoicing and delivery. Send them 2 or 3 short emails a week.
9. **Trade fairs.** If one trip becomes possible, plan around **Internorga (Hamburg, March)** for food service and **Bar Convent Berlin (October)** for bars. Even without a stand, you can pre-book meetings with distributors there.

## 2. P1: Cocktail bars

**Subject (DE):** Frischer Zitronen- & Limettensaft für [Bar name] – ohne Pressen
**Subject (EN):** Fresh lemon & lime juice for [Bar name], no squeezing

**Deutsch**
> Hallo [Vorname],
>
> ich bin [Name] von Citronista. Wir stellen [100 % / frisch gepressten / kaltgepressten] Zitronen- und Limettensaft speziell für Bars her.
>
> Für [Bar name] heißt das:
> – **kein tägliches Pressen** – ca. [X] Minuten weniger Prep pro Schicht
> – **gleicher Geschmack in jedem Sour**, egal wer hinter der Bar steht
> – **[X] Tage haltbar** geschlossen, [X] Tage nach Anbruch
> – ca. **[€ 0,XX] pro Drink** (bei [20/25] ml)
>
> Wir liefern per Kurier direkt zu euch, ab [Mindestmenge].
>
> Darf ich euch diese Woche **2 Flaschen kostenlos** zum Testen schicken? Ein kurzes „Ja“ mit Lieferadresse reicht.
>
> Viele Grüße
> [Name] · Citronista · [Telefon/WhatsApp] · citronista.com

**English**
> Hi [First name],
>
> I'm [Name] from Citronista. We make [100% / fresh-pressed / cold-pressed] lemon and lime juice for bars.
>
> For [Bar name] that means no daily squeezing (about [X] minutes saved per shift), the same taste in every sour whoever is behind the bar, [X] days shelf life sealed, and roughly [€0.XX] per drink.
>
> We deliver by courier straight to your door, from [minimum order].
>
> Can I send you 2 free bottles to try this week? Just reply "yes" with the delivery address.
>
> Best,
> [Name]

*Tip for priority-C award-winning bars:* don't suggest they stop squeezing. Pitch Citronista as **back-up for busy nights, for batching, pop-ups and events**. If they say yes, you have a strong reference to quote to everyone else.

## 8. Follow-ups (all segments)

> Hallo [Vorname], kurze Nachfrage zu meiner Mail von [Tag] – soll ich euch 2 Gratis-Flaschen Citronista zum Testen schicken? Ein „Ja“ + Adresse genügt. 🍋

**Follow-up 1, EN**
> Hi [First name], quick follow-up on my email from [day]: shall I send you 2 free bottles of Citronista to try? A "yes" and your address is all I need. 🍋

**Follow-up 2 (+10 days), DE (the "break-up" email, which often gets the most replies)**
> Hallo [Vorname], ich möchte nicht nerven – ist Zitrus-Saft gerade kein Thema für euch, oder bin ich bei der falschen Person? Falls jemand anderes den Einkauf macht, freue ich mich über einen kurzen Hinweis. Danke!

**Follow-up 2, EN**
> Hi [First name], I don't want to be a pest. Is citrus juice just not a priority for you right now, or have I reached the wrong person? If someone else handles purchasing, a quick pointer would be great. Thanks!

**After a sample arrives (+3 days)**

## 10. Weekly rhythm (no on-site visits needed)

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Mon | 15 new emails or DMs (start with priority A) |
| Tue | Follow-up 1 to last week's list; send samples to everyone who said yes |
| Wed | 2–3 distributor emails; LinkedIn connection requests to F&B and purchasing managers |
| Thu | Follow-up 2; call people who received samples |
| Fri | Update the spreadsheet; post 1 Instagram reel of a bartender using Citronista |

Working all 100 leads takes about 7 weeks. At realistic rates (15–25% replies once you follow up and offer samples), expect **15–25 conversations and 15+ sample tests** from this list.
